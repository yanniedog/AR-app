# Scoped eligibility v2

The product route can lazily assess independently reviewed criteria for a product or exact source rate variants. Products without rate rows are accessible through the searchable details catalogue; no rate row is invented. This capability returns eligibility traces only, not monetary results or bank approval. Absent reviewed assets remain unavailable.

## Publication authority

The URL-free `manifest.executable_v2` namespace sits outside `files`, whose optional descriptors the published 1.0.203 reader downloads eagerly. New requests derive immutable repository/tag/filename URLs, validate frozen schemas, filename identities, the shared 8 MiB transfer inventory, and the recomputed bundle identity. No request participates in core adoption. The producer retains the actual shipped-reader compatibility evidence.

Selections require original verified core and details objects, exact manifest/core/details digests, source observation and export-contract association, exact product-record/rate-row hashes, approval subject equality, and unique subject/scope identities. A product key or copied selection object grants no authority. Scope dates are reviewed assessment coverage, not inferred bank policy dates.

## Private execution

`evaluateEligibilitySelection` is the `scoped-eligibility-v1` adapter for evaluator v8. Assessment date and declared scenario roles come only from the current local scenario. They never fall back to saved customer answers. Customer facts use scoped encrypted profile answers; decisive rule branches determine relevant missing questions. Zero/false and unavailable/not-applicable retain their meanings.

Every candidate is assessed independently. Overlap requires explicit choice and never merges criteria. Date, input, profile or edition changes invalidate displayed results. Export rechecks the selection and assessment date. The bounded receipt retains the exact hashed evaluation input, only the selected scope's customer answers, source/approval identities, resolved typed facts and the complete eligibility trace.

Technical fixtures and adapter bridge outputs establish protocol behavior only. No live source subject or bank contract is activated by this code. Existing TD v1 transport and monetary evaluator behavior are unchanged.
